    USER GUIDE
    A few simple steps.
    From your original file to a reviewed result.
    
      
    
        
    01
    Choose and preview your file
    Open Workspace, choose a CSV, XLSX, or XLS file, then select Preview file. Check the delimiter, text encoding, header row and Excel worksheet. Update the preview after changing settings. Confirm the columns and text, then select Confirm and analyze. The same interpretation is used for cleaning.
    
        
    02
    Review the findings
    Use Original data & findings to check row and column counts, quality findings, and a preview. If all fields appear in one column, check the CSV delimiter before cleaning.
    
        
    03
    Choose your changes
    Default Mode lists eligible recommendations with simple explanations. Uncheck any you do not want. Review Review choices for simple per-issue treatment choices. Row deletion starts unchecked. Expert Mode keeps its own detailed settings and selections. Each mode retains its own selection while you switch.
    
        
    04
    Download and verify
    Use the fixed bottom bar to preview your choices, then select Apply changes, then download the cleaned file, validation report, change log, original row mapping or column-type manifest. Filled and edited preview cells are labelled. Compare the result with your expectations. A successful request or validation status does not verify every value's accuracy.
    
      
    
      
    What do the impact labels mean?
    Safe means the planner considers an action low impact. Review means you should consider its effect, such as deleting duplicate rows. High impact marks changes that need extra care, including protected columns. These are recommendations, not guarantees.
    
      
    Will my session be saved?
    Your file selection and results stay available as you navigate these three pages. Refreshing or closing the browser clears the interface session. Files and outputs expire after the configured retention period (24 hours by default). Saved plans remain until you delete them. They belong to this browser session; clearing cookies loses access. This is not an account or cross-device login.
    
      
    How do remembered decisions work?
    Review column meanings and add rules, then confirm them. Similar labels require an explicit equivalence decision. Preview your selected changes, name the plan and approve saving. On another file, open Saved plans to check compatibility and load a plan for review. Statistical fills are recalculated from the new data. Delete a plan whenever it is no longer useful.
